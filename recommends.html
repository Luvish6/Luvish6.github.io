All-time favourite

The Big Bang Theory

Sitcom · 12 seasons

Four brilliant, socially awkward scientists and the friends who put up with them. Endlessly rewatchable, and Sheldon never gets old.

💼

Suits

Legal drama · 9 seasons

A college dropout with a photographic memory talks his way into a top law firm. Sharp dialogue, big wins, and a lot of strategy. A bit like chess with briefcases.

🔍

The Mentalist

Crime drama · 7 seasons

A former fake psychic uses observation and reading people to solve crimes. Great if you like noticing the small details everyone else misses.
