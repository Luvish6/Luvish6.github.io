Major project · in progress

Mining Disturbance & River Degradation Monitoring

My final-year major project: multi-sensor satellite monitoring of mining-related land disturbance and downstream river degradation in the East and West Jaintia Hills of Meghalaya (about 3,800 km²). It combines Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical imagery in Google Earth Engine, with seasonal change features, a Random Forest evaluation pipeline, a Streamlit dashboard and a React review portal.
